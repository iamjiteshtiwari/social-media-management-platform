Important behavior

What these actions should do when connected

  • Suspend: deny protected access and revoke active sessions; retain account records for review.
  • Disable: block sign-in until an authorized administrator reactivates the account.
  • Request deletion: use a confirmation and documented retention period before anonymizing or removing personal data.
  • Confirm closure: soft-delete/anonymize first, record an audit event, and never rely on a frontend-only status.
